# Branding masters

Original logo artwork, kept as the source for the optimised files in `public/images/`.

| Master                             | Use                             | Served as                                            |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| `testd-logo-light-bg.original.png` | Dark ink, for light backgrounds | `testd-logo-light-bg.png`, `testd-mark-light-bg.png` |
| `testd-logo-dark-bg.original.png`  | Light ink, for dark backgrounds | `testd-logo-dark-bg.png`, `testd-mark-dark-bg.png`   |

The served files are cropped to the artwork, stripped of faint background haze and stray specks, and
downscaled (logo 120 px high, mark 96 px high). The mark is the "T" with its check.
Do not reference the masters from the app; they are about 500 KB each.

The browser icon master is `favicon.original.png` (1254 px). It is cropped to its artwork and served as
`testd-favicon.png` (32 px), `testd-icon-192.png`, `testd-apple-touch-icon.png` (180 px) and `favicon.ico` (16, 32 and 48 px).
